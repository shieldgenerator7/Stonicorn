Stonicorn - Things to know


# Teleport
- teleport in any direction
- teleport limited by range
- limited number of teleports
- can rotate by teleporting to same position (or pressing rotate button)
- can't teleport into walls
- range reduced when teleporting straight up into air
- goes on cooldown when teleporting straight up into air
- range refreshed upon hitting the ground
- in certain situations, can teleport beyond shown range
- teleport range indicator shows range, cooldown, teleports left
- you maintain momentum when teleporting
- teleporting in opposite direction of momentum slows you down

# Merky
- solid object
- doesn't move
- rotates off ledges
- can break some things by slamming into them

# Time Rewind
- automatically prompts to go back to start when run out of teleports
- you can zoom in/out
- zooming out shows past merkys
- select past merky to go there
- zooming out pauses the game
- selecting the background cancels the time rewind
- auto-triggers when merky hits a hazard
- rewinding to a past merky refreshes your teleport range
- teleporting normally creates a save state
- teleporting in air (with limited range) doesn't create a save state
- select the background during a time rewind to cancel it

# Flashlight
- you can pan the camera
- panning the camera goes into plan mode
- plan mode shows flashlight
- flashlight reveals things in dark areas
- flashlight reveals fake platforms
- flashlight (temporarily) reveals hidden areas
- you can teleport into hidden areas that you are revealing with the flashlight

# Speed-Port
- you can hold teleport to gain momentum in the direction you teleport
- letting go launches you in the direction youre pointing when you let go

# Tree Climb
- you're grounded in the direction your feet are pointing, if there is a solid object there
- you're not grounded if your feet are pointing at a hazard

# Air-Port
- teleporting straight down or to the side doesn't limit your teleport range or make it go on cooldown, even when teleporting into empty space

# Swap
- teleport into an object to swap with it
- you can only teleport with objects relatively the same size as you

# Long Teleport
- you can set your teleport range by panning the camera while in a checkpoint
- this range set lasts until you rewind out of it, or you set it again

# Checkpoints
- teleporting into or hitting a checkpoint makes it active
- while inside a checkpoint, and at least two checkpoints are active, select a preview to teleport to it
- there are slots in a checkpoint, and teleporting to a different checkpoint keeps you in that same slot
