Stonicorn - Things to know


# Teleport
- teleport in any direction
- teleport limited by range
- limited number of teleports
- can rotate by teleporting to same position (or pressing rotate button)
- can't teleport into walls
- can teleport past walls
- cant teleport when not grounded
- teleport refreshed upon hitting the ground
- in certain situations, can teleport beyond shown range
- you maintain momentum when teleporting
- teleporting in opposite direction of momentum slows you down

# Merky
- solid object
- doesn't move
- rotates off ledges
- can break some things by slamming into them

# Time Rewind
- automatically prompts to go back to start when run out of teleports
- always shows past merkys that are on the edge of the screen
- select past merky to go back to that point in time
- auto-triggers when merky hits a hazard
- manually rewinding to a past merky refreshes your teleport
- manually rewinding to a past merky increases your teleport range for your next teleport based on how far back you teleported
- teleporting normally creates a save state
- manual rotate doesn't create a save state
- tap during a manual time rewind to stop the rewind early

# Flashlight
- when hold/drag, show flashlight and teleport preview after a delay
- teleport preview shows where merky will teleport
- flashlight shows where cursor is at
- camera pans in the direction of the flashlight area
- camera returns to merky when flashlight turns off
- flashlight reveals things in dark areas
- flashlight reveals fake platforms
- hold to slow down time
- release to teleport to the preview
- right-click or double tap to cancel the teleport

# Flame Dash
- teleport quickly 3 times in the same direction to dash in the direction you teleport
- the 3 teleports have to be near base teleport range, cant be close up
- dash has only initial burst of momentum that decays naturally
- while dashing, teleport in your moving direction to refresh the dash (another burst of momentum)
- while dashing, teleport in your moving direction to slice through cuttable objects
- while dashing, teleport opposite your moving direction to cancel momentum
- while dashing, you bounce of walls
- while dashing, youre on fire
- while dashing, hazards deal 1 less damage (minimum 0)

# Wall Cling
- when merky has his feet against a solid non-hazard surface, he clings to it
- while clinging, merky is grounded
- clinging refreshes teleport
- while clinging, merky is immune to gravity
- while clinging, merky moves with the solid objects velocity (usually 0 for walls)
- the initial cling cancels all momentum and rotates merky to cling to the surface the best

# Air-Port
- you can teleport in your moving direction when not grounded

# Swap
- teleport into an object to swap with it
- you can only teleport with objects relatively the same size and dimensions as you
- you also swap rotations
- swapping with an object refreshes your teleport
- swapping down into an object so the object ends up in the air above you allows you to telekinesis it
- telekinesis objects get dragged to their relative position near you, allowing you to take them with you
- telekinesis objects get dropped when you get too far away or they come into contact with another solid object

# Long Teleport
- manually rewinding to a past merky increases your teleport range for your next teleport based on how far back you teleported

# Battery
- standing on wires powers them
- standing on wires refreshes your teleport

# Checkpoints
- teleporting into or hitting a checkpoint makes it active
- while inside a checkpoint, and at least two checkpoints are active, select a preview to teleport to it
- each checkpoint has a set other checkpoint it can show in the slots, each slot is for a specific checkpoint
- entering checkpoint gives you a 1 time use shield
- manually rewinding into a checkpoint makes your increased range last until you rewind again
