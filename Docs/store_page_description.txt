Description
In the end times, a stone unicorn wakes up in a dark cave. What happened and where is everyone?

Stonicorn is a 2D puzzle/platformer where you play as Merky, a stone unicorn trying to escape a doomed planet. Being stone, you can only teleport. Explore the world, meet NPCs, and find and upgrade all 6 abilities. And finally, get to the moon!

This is game is a work in progress, and has lots of bugs, but is playable from start to finish. Check back annually for new updates!

Gameplay Facts:
Supported Devices: Mouse.
Touch, Keyboard, and Controller support coming eventually.

Mouse input involves:
Clicking the Left Mouse Button (LMB)
Rapidly Clicking LMB
Clicking and Holding LMB
Moving the mouse while Holding LMB
Moving the mouse while Holding RMB
Using the scroll wheel
