Description
In the end times, a stone unicorn awakens in a dark cave. How did he get here? Where is everyone? Where should he go? What should he do? (How can he save it?)

Stonicorn is a 2D puzzle/platformer where walking and jumping are out of the question. Being stone, you can only teleport. Expand your teleportation abilities and learn new skills to save the unicorns and their planet!

Explore the world, meet NPCs, and find and acquire all 6 abilities. And finally, get to the moon!

This is only the demo version of the intended full game. Check back annually for new updates.

Gameplay Facts:
Supported Devices: Touch and Mouse.
Keyboard and Controller support coming eventually.

Touch input involves:
Tapping the screen
Rapidly Tapping the screen
Touching and Holding on the screen
Dragging on the screen
2-Finger Pinching on the screen
Mouse input involves:
Clicking the Left Mouse Button (LMB)
Rapidly Clicking LMB
Clicking and Holding LMB
Moving the mouse while Holding LMB
Moving the mouse while Holding RMB
Using the scroll wheel
